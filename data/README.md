# Data

`data/damodaran/` holds three industry datasets by Aswath Damodaran (NYU Stern, January 2026 update), used only by
`scripts/calibration_check.py` to check the sector driver ranges against published industry averages
(`docs/research/04_calibration.md`). They are not redistributed here; download them from
<https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pc/datasets/>:

- `margin.xls` (operating and EBITDA margins by industry)
- `capex.xls` (capital expenditures and depreciation by industry)
- `wcdata.xls` (working capital ratios by industry)

No other data is used: every task, workbook and reference answer is generated by the code.
